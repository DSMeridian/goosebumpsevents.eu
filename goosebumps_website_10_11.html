About Goosebumps

The Art of Unforgettable Events

Goosebumps is Europe's premier lighting, sound and entertainment service — built for occasions where ordinary simply isn't an option. We provide complete stage technology, precision sound engineering, and breathtaking lighting design for the most exclusive private events across the continent.

Our clients include a special niche of families where traditions and values are still considered important, and cultural institutions who demand not just excellence, but absolute discretion. Every event is handled with the most professionalism.

What We Offer

Our Services

From intimate dinners to grand European galas — we handle every technical element so you can focus on creating memories. One call. Total coverage.

01

Premium Sound

Crystal-clear audio for any space — from intimate salons to grand ballrooms. We use top-tier PA systems tested for the best quality, live mixing consoles to make all necessary changes, and bespoke acoustic solutions tailored to every unique venue so we can promise the best quality possible.

02

Lighting Design

Atmospheric lighting that transforms any space. We programm a special and unique light show for your event only. Programmed by a proffessional lighting designer and deleted after the event. This way every event will be unique and special.

03

DJ & Live Music

Our DJs are carefully selected; each DJ must meet certain criteria to be allowed to perform at your event under our name. They are also all assessed on their ability to read the crowd. This ensures that the music played is always what the audience wants. Our live saxophone option adds a fantastic extra touch to make the party truly unforgettable.

04

Stage Technology

Complete stage build, rigging, power distribution and technical direction. Our experienced crew handles every technical aspect so not a single cable is out of place.

05

Special Effects

Smoke – fog machines, confetti cannons, flamethrowers, lasers and custom pyrotechnics — We have specialists in special effects. Thanks to them, the lasers will shoot stunning beams through the smoke, the flame throwers will shoot 4-metre-high flames into the air at just the right moments, and the confetti will fly when the moment is perfect.

06

Full Production

One trusted partner for everything. We manage the complete technical production of your event — absolute discretion, flawless execution from setup to final breakdown. We will provide everything.

How We Work

From Vision to Reality

01

Initial Consultation

We listen to your vision, understand the venue, the guest profile and the exact atmosphere you want to achieve — in complete confidentiality.

02

Bespoke Proposal

We design a fully tailored production plan — lighting scheme, sound architecture, entertainment programme and effects — crafted specifically for your event.

03

Seamless Setup

Our team arrives early, sets up with military precision, and conducts full technical rehearsals. Your guests will never see the machinery — only the magic.

04

Flawless Delivery

On the night, our technical director manages every cue in real time. After the event, we pack down quietly and completely — leaving only memories.
